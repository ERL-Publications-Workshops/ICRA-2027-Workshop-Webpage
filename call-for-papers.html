ICRA 2027 · PROPOSED WORKSHOP

Call for Papers.

New results, field experience and open challenges in small-scale underwater manipulation.

CONTRIBUTIONS

Share what you’re
working on.

The planned call will invite two-page abstracts on new results, field experiments, integration and failure cases, datasets, and application requirements.

Topics of interest

  • Hardware, end-effectors and system integration
  • Underwater perception, planning and control
  • Shared autonomy, field reliability and recovery
  • Datasets, benchmarks and application needs

The proposal plans stage time for every accepted contribution, through a five-minute lightning talk or a two-minute poster pitch, alongside the poster session.
